# Intellectual-property boundary

Brilyetz Holding S.L. owns the original GOVP materials identified by the
repository history and NOTICE. GOVP is deliberately licensed under Apache-2.0;
recipients receive the rights in that license, including its scoped patent
grant. Gemacode and GOVP trademarks are governed separately by `TRADEMARKS.md`.

## GOVP and proprietary products

GOVP defines open records, schemas, validation, causal/witness mechanisms and
neutral conformance behavior. It does not contain or require proprietary
regulatory policy engines, enforcement runtimes, one-use authorization,
evidence-sufficiency decisions, certification, audit operations or commercial
domain packs. A product may implement or consume GOVP without contributing its
separate proprietary implementation to this repository.

No submission may copy QEL or other proprietary product code, tests, internal
designs, customer material or confidential information into GOVP. A compatible
wire interface is not permission to publish its proprietary implementation.

## Patent-sensitive publication

Apache-2.0 section 3 grants patent rights for contributor claims necessarily
infringed by an accepted contribution. A submission that may disclose an
invention, claim implementation or unpublished patent-sensitive mechanism must
not be opened as an issue or pull request until the rights owner records an
`OPEN` or `PATENT_FILED_AND_LICENSE_APPROVED` publication decision. Unresolved
material is `BLOCKED`.

The project does not provide a freedom-to-operate opinion. Contributors remain
responsible for identifying third-party material and known rights that may
affect their submission.

## Incoming rights

The DCO records the submitter's right to contribute under Apache-2.0 but does
not transfer ownership. Code is merged only from a contributor whose employment
assignment or separately executed contributor/corporate IP agreement has been
verified by Brilyetz Holding S.L. The private agreement reference is recorded
by the maintainer; the agreement itself is not published.

Feedback and defect reports that contain no code may be accepted without an IP
agreement. If GOVP later chooses to accept Apache-only external contributions
without assignment, governance must expressly acknowledge that those
contributions have separate copyright owners and that Brilyetz Holding S.L. can
no longer claim sole ownership of the combined work.
